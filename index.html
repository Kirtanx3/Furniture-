Inquiries & Care

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our commissioning process, wood behavior, and white-glove logistics.

How does natural solid hardwood respond to seasonal changes?

Real wood breathes and naturally expands or contracts fractionally with seasonal humidity shifts. We kiln-cure all lumber down to 6–8% moisture equilibrium and engineer our tables with slotted architectural expansion fasteners and stabilizer C-channels embedded underneath. This gives the wood room to breathe without ever bowing or warping.

Can I request specific wood slab character or exact custom sizing?

Yes! While our interactive 3D configurator lets you customize down to the centimeter, you can also book a video consultation with our head timber scout. We can share photographs of raw flitch slabs currently in our drying kilns so you can hand-select the exact tree trunk for your piece.

What is your finish maintenance routine?

Our plant-based hardwax oil penetrates deep into the wood fibers rather than forming an artificial plastic film on top. Everyday cleaning requires only a damp cloth with mild soap. Every commission arrives with our complimentary Atelier Care Kit containing our organic maintenance oil and a wool burnishing cloth.

How does White-Glove In-Home Delivery work?

All pieces travel in climate-controlled padded timber crates. Our white-glove two-person delivery team carries the furniture to your room of choice, assembles the base, levels the feet, removes all packaging, and polishes the top before departing.
